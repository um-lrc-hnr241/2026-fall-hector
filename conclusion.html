Conclusion

Awaken

The question raised by Westworld is not just whether a host can become conscious, but what it takes for a life to become one’s own.

The series connects awakening with memory, self-understanding, and the capacity to question an imposed narrative. Read alongside Charles Larmore’s philosophical work on the self, it invites us to consider how a person makes sense of their experience and how that perspective relates to agency.

The project’s takeaway is a question rather than a simple test for consciousness: when someone can interpret their past, understand themselves as continuing through time, and challenge the story assigned to them, what responsibilities do others have toward them? Westworld does not make that question easy, and that difficulty is part of its force.

A student project on Westworld, consciousness, and the self.
