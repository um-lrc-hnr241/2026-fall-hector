OUR
INVESTIGATION

In our Honors 241 project, we use Charles Larmore’s philosophy of the self to explore Westworld Season One and its questions about consciousness, identity, and what it means to understand a life as one’s own. Across eight Builds, our essays develop these connections by examining how memory, self-reflection, and agency shape the hosts’ emerging sense of self.
