Charles Larmore

Charles Larmore is a philosopher whose work considers ethics, political thought, and the self.

The self and self-understanding

In The Practices of the Self, Larmore examines how people understand themselves and make sense of their lives. His work provides a philosophical point of entry for this project’s questions about consciousness, identity, and agency.

Read alongside Westworld, these ideas help us ask how memory and reflection shape a sense of self. The comparison is a way to explore the series’ questions, not a claim that its fictional hosts prove a particular theory of consciousness.
